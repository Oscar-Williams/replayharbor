# Fixed-sample confirmation and revision comparison

Protocol implemented before the new workflow run, 2026-09-28. Original smoke runs remain unchanged.

## Scope and units

The estimand is failure probability conditional on one fixed task, saved state and replay policy. A replay outcome is boolean task failure. Repeated replays describe one case, not multiple independent users or tasks. Seeds are generated by a deterministic PRNG for reproducibility; seed separation alone does not prove statistical independence. Inference assumes independent draws from a fixed replay distribution and is conditional on that assumption.

## Candidate confirmation

Freeze the exploration-selected candidate k. Before observing confirmation outcomes, fix n=512 per prefix and alpha=0.05. Collect fresh seed-disjoint samples at 0 and k. For each failure proportion, use radius sqrt(log(4/alpha)/(2n)), clipped to [0,1]. Hoeffding's inequality and a union bound give simultaneous coverage for these two proportions under the stated assumptions. Subtract interval endpoints to bound pk−p0.

Supported: lower bound of pk meets p_fail and lower bound of pk−p0 meets delta. Not supported: the corresponding upper bound rules out either requirement. Other outcomes are inconclusive. The result concerns one frozen candidate; earlier prefixes and causal root cause remain unconfirmed. Across multiple cases or repeated confirmation attempts, alpha does not provide a family-wide guarantee. Further experiments must retain earlier outcomes and specify their comparison family.

Partial samples after errors produce no inferential classification. A budget below 2n triggers zero confirmation calls. Exploration and confirmation budgets are shown separately and summed in the report.

## Revision comparison

The user fixes the intervention, prefix and n=128 pairs before the run. Each pair uses the same seed before and after. Let D=before_failure−after_failure in [-1,1]. The mean D measures failure-rate reduction. Radius sqrt(2 log(2/alpha)/n) gives a two-sided Hoeffding bound for independent pairs. Within-pair dependence is intentional. An interval wholly above zero supports improvement for this case; wholly below zero supports regression; otherwise report inconclusive.

The default `clarify` intervention is an upstream scripted change. It does not establish the performance of a generated patch, a language model, or a production agent. New failure signatures are not yet collected separately; regressed pairs measure newly failing task outcomes only.

## Method source and review

[Stanford concentration notes](https://ai.stanford.edu/~gwthomas/notes/concentration.html), accessed 2026-09-28, state the two-sided bounded-independent-variable inequality used above. The formulas here specialize it to Bernoulli outcomes and paired differences. These conservative bounds were selected for explicit assumptions and a dependency-free implementation; efficiency comparisons with alternative intervals remain future work.

Statistical reporting review used the nature-statistics skill: units, sampling, multiplicity, assumptions and claim boundaries were checked. No p-value, production accuracy or generalization claim is produced.

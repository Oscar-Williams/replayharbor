# Community conduct

Treat participants with respect. Give specific, constructive feedback, assume good faith, and welcome different experience levels. Keep discussions relevant and accessible.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, disclosure of private information and deliberate disruption are unacceptable. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and restrict participation. Use GitHub's report-abuse tools for private reports; omit personal information from public issues. Decisions should prioritize participant safety and a productive technical community.
